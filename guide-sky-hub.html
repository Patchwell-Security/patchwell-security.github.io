How to secure your Sky hub

Five steps, about 20 minutes. No technical knowledge needed.

Covers the Sky Max Hub, Sky Gigafast+ Hub and older Sky Broadband Hubs. Checked against Sky's own help pages on 23 November 2026.

Your hub is the front door between your business and the internet. Sky hubs have one setting that catches a lot of people out, and step 2 fixes it.

Which hub do you have? Look at the label on the bottom. Newer Sky Max and Gigafast+ hubs are managed mostly in the My Sky app. Older Sky hubs are managed from a settings page in your web browser. Each step below covers both.

  1. Find your hub's details

    Your Wi-Fi name and password are on the bottom of the hub and on the "Keep me handy" card that came with it. The hub's settings page uses the username admin.

  2. Change the hub admin password

    This is the important one. On Sky Max and Gigafast+ hubs, the admin password starts out the same as the Wi-Fi password on the bottom of the hub. That means anyone you have ever given the Wi-Fi password to could also get into your hub's settings.

    On a computer connected to your Sky broadband:

    1. Type 192.168.0.1 into your browser's address bar and press Enter.
    2. Sign in with username admin and the Wi-Fi password from the bottom of the hub.
    3. Select Troubleshooting → Change password.
    4. Enter the current password in Old Password, then your new one in New Password and Repeat New Password. Select Save.

    Older Sky hubs: sign in at the same address with username admin and the password sky or your Wi-Fi password, then look for the password option in the Maintenance menu.

    A good password is three random words joined together, plus a number. Keep it in a password manager or somewhere private, and make it different from your Wi-Fi password.

  3. Change the Wi-Fi password

    The Wi-Fi password on the hub has been seen by everyone who has been near it. Changing it means only the people you choose can join.

    Sky Max or Gigafast+: open the My Sky app and tap Manage → Broadband (under Package) → Product settings → your hub's name. If your hub isn't listed, you may need to finish setting it up in the app first.

    Older Sky hubs: at 192.168.0.1, select Wireless Settings (it may be called Setup or Wireless), scroll to the security settings, change the password and select Apply.

    Then reconnect each of your devices with the new password. Avoid putting your business name or address in the Wi-Fi name.

  4. Check your Wi-Fi security level

    On Sky Max and Gigafast+ hubs, in the My Sky app go to Manage → Broadband → WiFi Settings → Advanced settings → WiFi security level. Choose the strongest option your devices can all connect with. If an older device stops connecting, step down one level.

  5. Leave port forwarding and DMZ switched off

    The same Advanced settings menu has Port forwarding and DMZ. These open doors through your hub's protection from the internet. Unless a trusted supplier has told you exactly why you need one, make sure nothing is set up there.

Your quick checklist

  • Admin password changed, and different from the Wi-Fi password
  • Wi-Fi password changed, and all devices reconnected
  • Wi-Fi name doesn't include your business name or address
  • Strongest Wi-Fi security level your devices support
  • No port forwarding or DMZ unless you know why it's there
  • "Keep me handy" card stored somewhere private

Forgotten your admin password?

You'll need to reset the hub. This puts every setting back to the details on the bottom of the hub, including the Wi-Fi password, so repeat this guide afterwards.

  • Sky Max Hub: press and hold the WPS button on the side for 30 seconds.
  • Sky Gigafast+ Hub: press and hold the Reset button on the back until the front light flashes green.
  • Older Sky hubs: hold the reset button on the back for about 10 seconds, until the power light flashes.

Wait a few minutes for the hub to restart before checking your broadband.

Changing default passwords and not opening unnecessary routes in from the internet are part of the basic controls in the UK's Cyber Essentials scheme. Our Cyber Essentials Readiness Kit (£25) walks you through all five controls, step by step.
