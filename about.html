About

Security help for people who don't do IT.

Patchwell Security is a small UK business that turns cyber security advice into simple steps anyone can follow.

Who it's for

Sole traders, freelancers and small businesses with between 1 and 10 people. If you run a trade, a shop, a salon or a small office and nobody looks after your IT, this is for you.

What you get

  • Free guides that walk you through one job at a time, such as securing your router.
  • Downloadable kits and templates for bigger jobs, such as getting ready for Cyber Essentials.
  • Updates when the rules or the threats change, so your setup stays right.

What we don't do

  • No sales calls and no consultants. Everything is written down for you to follow at your own pace.
  • No certification. Cyber Essentials certificates are only issued by accredited certification bodies. Our kits help you prepare to apply.
All guidance is checked against official advice from the UK's National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) before it is published.
