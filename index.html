Five layers that stop most attacks

Most attacks on small businesses are not clever. They get in through an old router password, a missed update or a fake invoice email. The UK government's Cyber Essentials scheme focuses on five basic controls. Get these right and you are protected from the most common attacks.

LAYER 1

Firewalls

Your router and devices only let in the traffic you expect.

LAYER 2

Secure settings

Default passwords changed, unused accounts and apps removed.

LAYER 3

Updates

Software and devices kept up to date, so known holes are closed.

LAYER 4

User access

Everyone has their own login, strong passwords and two-step sign-in.

LAYER 5

Malware protection

Built-in protection switched on and only trusted apps installed.
