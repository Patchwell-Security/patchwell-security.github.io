How to secure your Virgin Media Hub

Six steps, about 20 minutes. No technical knowledge needed.

Written for the Virgin Media Hub 5. The Hub 3 and Hub 4 use the same address and very similar menus. Checked against Virgin Media's own help pages on 23 November 2026.

Your hub is the front door between your business and the internet. Every laptop, card reader, phone and printer goes through it. These steps make sure only you can change its settings and only the right people can join your network.

Before you start: use a laptop or computer connected to the hub. Changing the Wi-Fi password will disconnect every device, so pick a quiet time and have your devices to hand.

  1. Find the labels on the bottom of the hub

    Turn the hub over. The label shows your Wi-Fi name, Wi-Fi password and a separate settings password.

    The settings password is not the Wi-Fi password. It's the one that unlocks the hub's settings page.

  2. Open the hub's settings page

    Type 192.168.0.1 into your browser's address bar and press Enter, then sign in with the settings password from the label.

    If someone has switched your hub to Modem Mode (for example to use a separate router), use 192.168.100.1 instead.

  3. Change the settings password

    This is the most important step: anyone who knows the settings password can change everything on your hub.

    Select Admin → Change password. Enter the current settings password, then your new one, and select Apply changes.

    Use three random words joined together, plus a number, and keep it somewhere private. If your hub asked you to change this password when it was first set up, check that whoever did it has passed the new one to you.

  4. Change the Wi-Fi password

    Select Advanced settings → Wireless → Security. Enter a new Wi-Fi password, and keep the same network name for 2.4GHz and 5GHz so the password applies to both. Select Apply changes, then reconnect your devices.

    Avoid putting your business name or address in the Wi-Fi name.

  5. Give visitors the guest network

    Virgin Media hubs can run a separate guest network, which you can set up from the same settings page. If customers or visitors ever need Wi-Fi, give them the guest network, never your main one. It keeps their devices away from your laptop, printer and card reader.

  6. Keep the hub out of visitors' reach

    The hub has a WPS button that lets a device join without typing a password. Anyone standing next to the hub can use it. Keep the hub somewhere customers can't reach, and if your hub's Wireless settings show a WPS option you don't use, turn it off.

    Leave the firewall and port settings as they are, unless a trusted supplier tells you exactly why to change them.

Your quick checklist

  • Settings password changed from the one on the label
  • Wi-Fi password changed, and all devices reconnected
  • Wi-Fi name doesn't include your business name or address
  • Visitors use the guest network, not the main one
  • Hub kept out of customers' reach
  • Firewall and port settings left alone

Forgotten your new settings password?

Reset the hub: press the pinhole button with the tip of a pen for 10 seconds, then leave it for 5–10 minutes to start up. It's ready when the power light is solid white. This puts every setting back to the label, including the Wi-Fi password, so repeat this guide afterwards.
